## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are developers and operators evaluating or adopting ChainBot from a landing page context. They are trying to quickly understand whether the CLI is reliable enough to replace shell-script glue for trigger-driven workflow orchestration.

### Brand Personality
Refined, trustworthy, operator-grade. The interface should feel calm, competent, and technically credible rather than flashy or startup-hyped.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the existing warm-light plus green-accent palette. Typography should lean toward refined tooling: editorial enough to feel intentional, but restrained enough to support product trust. Avoid generic SaaS typography, loud gradients, and novelty-driven display choices.

### Design Principles
1. Make trust legible before making the page dramatic.
2. Use typography to create calm hierarchy, not decorative noise.
3. Keep command surfaces precise, compact, and operator-readable.
4. Preserve warmth in the palette while keeping interaction patterns disciplined.
5. Prefer one coherent visual language over multiple competing accents.
